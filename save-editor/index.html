    Atlas override

    Point the World workspace at a modded game data/ folder. Pick the six atlas files (terrain.png/.xml, locs.png/.xml, sprites.png/.xml). Any you leave out keep the bundled version.

    TMX import

    Replace every tile from a CSV-encoded TMX layer. Tiles only — locations and buried items have their own editors here, so they’re left alone.

    The TMX must match the loaded map's dimensions. Growth stages reset and mountain variants are re-randomised, because TMX stores neither.
